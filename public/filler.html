Filler reads the job posting you're looking at, drafts a cover letter tailored to it, and fills the application form for you — company, position, contact details, résumé upload, all of it.

It runs entirely in your browser. There's no backend server: your OpenAI key stays local and talks directly to OpenAI, and your profile, résumé, and cover letter drafts live in your own Google Drive — not on a server Filler's developer controls.

What it does

  • →

    Extracts company, position, requirements, and tech stack from the page you're on

  • →

    Drafts a cover letter grounded in your CV and a "Personal Legend" you write once — not generic filler, and it won't invent experience you don't have

  • →

    Autofills application forms by matching field labels to your profile, across languages and site layouts

  • →

    Lets you drag any single value — name, email, a custom field, the cover letter itself — straight onto the page

  • →

    Answers custom application questions ("Why do you want to work here?") using your CV, cover letter, and the job description as context

  • →

    Translates the cover letter for your own reading, kept in sync as you edit

  • →

    Remembers your CV, profile, and drafts per application, so switching tabs never loses your work

How it's built

Manifest V3 React TipTap

Your data goes to exactly two places, both authenticated with your own credentials: OpenAI (your API key) and your own Google Drive app-data folder.

No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data sharing. Full details in the privacy policy.
